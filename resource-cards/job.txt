Job Summary

The Dynamics 365 Developer is responsible for the architecture, design, development, and administration of AEG's Power Apps and Dynamics platform. This role is a critical resource focused on building, maintaining and supporting custom application versions across multiple business units. The role will be an integral member of the AEG Global Technology team and liaise directly with stakeholders from various business areas to understand and support their application needs. The role will also work closely with the Infrastructure and Operations team to ensure that all standards and security requirements are met and delivered.

 

Essential Functions

Administration, development and support of Microsoft Platforms Apps across various AEG entities.
Provide support and administration of third party solutions.
Maintenance of custom configurations within the Power Platform environment and Dynamics Platform.
Development of integrations of data and functionality using the Power Platform (Power Automate and Power Apps)
Documenting and defining user requirements with product stakeholders, including flow charts, layouts and diagrams
Establishes best practice for support of and knowledge sharing of the functionality of the Dynamics 365 and Power Platform environment.
Support Azure Web App Servers
Work with the AEG Data Engineering and BI teams to ensure data integrity across the applications and datasets when using Dataverse.
Required Qualifications

BA/BS Degree (4-year) Computer Science or related technical area
2-4 years Experience with Dynamics 365, Office 365 and Microsoft Power Platform preferred
Experience working with Azure Functions and Logic Apps and Azure Web Services
Experience of working with various systems integrations, including APIs, stream data sources
Experience working with application managements lifecycle tools/source control, Power Apps
Experience of Application Development, using C#, .Net and SQL
Experience with Azure SQL Database
Experience with Scripting languages including HTML5, CSS, Node.js, and Javascript
Experience with Dataverse is preferred
Experience with Typescript preferred
Experience with Power Apps Pipeline preferred
Strong verbal and written communication skills and ability to interface with all levels of the organization
Possesses a balance of an analytical personality coupled with strong collaborative, communication, written and executive presentation skills

Competency in Analytical Problem Solving, Product & Technology Expertise, Project Management, Strategic Insights
